## Products
Toys, craft products, RC cars and miniature/vintage-style cars.

## Ordering flow
- **Buy Now** opens a new WhatsApp tab with the selected product, quantity, price and store details pre-filled.
- **Add** adds the product to the local cart.
- The cart supports multiple products and quantities.
- **Send cart to WhatsApp** opens a new WhatsApp tab with the complete order message.
- No customer form or account is required.
