Common questions about Fancy Corner Online products, availability, pricing and ordering. If your question is not covered here, contact us directly.

Are the product images from Fancy Corner's actual collection?
Yes. The catalogue uses the product images supplied for Fancy Corner Online. Availability can change, so please confirm the item with us before ordering.
How do I know the price of a product?
The catalogue is enquiry-first. Select a product or send its shared product link to us on WhatsApp, and we will confirm the current price and availability.
Can I share a product from the website?
Yes. Use the share button on any product card. The link opens the matching product and automatically highlights it in the catalogue.
How do I place an order?
Browse the catalogue, select the products you want, then use the WhatsApp enquiry flow. We will confirm availability, pricing and the next steps directly.
Do you offer delivery?
Please contact Fancy Corner Online with your delivery location. Delivery availability, charges and timing are confirmed based on the order and destination.
Can I ask about a product that is not listed?
Yes. Send us the product name or a reference image on WhatsApp and we can check whether it is available or can be sourced.
